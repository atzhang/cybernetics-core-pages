Chart definitions

Subscription cost estimate

Task cost × monthly plan price ÷ (calibrated API-equivalent dollars per weekly limit × 4.348 weeks/month). Weeks/month uses the average Gregorian year: 365.2425 ÷ 12 ÷ 7. Assumes full weekly quota use; this is an allocated subscription estimate, not a per-task charge or guaranteed savings.

Frozen usage-dashboard calibration through 2026-10-26 16:34 UTC: OpenAI Pro Lite $100/month, $250.33/full weekly limit (15 observed windows); Claude Max 20x $200/month, $2,311.29/full weekly limit (5 observed windows). These pooled plan rates are applied uniformly to each creator's models as a scenario, including historical models; model-specific quota rates and plan availability can differ. Lower utilization increases effective cost. Other providers retain API prices.

Both calibrations are qualified estimates. Codex has unpriced usage, an unknown provider and outlier windows; some cache costs are lower bounds. Claude has outlier windows, session reconciliation warnings, incomplete usage and partially bounded missing cost, stale headless rate events, telemetry coverage gaps and mismatches. Its uncertainty band covers rounding only, not week-to-week variation. This frozen snapshot does not refresh from your usage dashboard automatically.

The cost frontier, comparisons and profile use the displayed costs. Model admission and selection evidence retain their original API-cost basis. Ability scores and latency stay the same.
